Website review

A clearer path to sound, classes and training.

The rebuilt site retains SoundSpace’s identity and public content while improving navigation, image quality, reading comfort and access to live calendars.

Area reviewedFinding and refinement
NavigationThe long desktop header wrapped across rows. Classes, training and information now have clear groups and a mobile menu.
PhotographyOriginal uploaded images are saved without recompression. Separate responsive versions provide sharp pictures at appropriate screen sizes.
CalendarsClass and event calendars have wide, deliberate loading areas, with direct provider links and an option to expand.
Long pagesReadable text widths, balanced image sections and consistent headings make training and practitioner information easier to scan.
Contact detailsA template phone number has been replaced with the verified business number. The studio address and map directions are easy to find.
Training linksAn outdated October registration shortcut returned an error. It now leads to the current SoundBody training calendar.
Historical eventsPast event content remains available and is identified as an archive, with current events accessible through the live provider.
Forms and member accessNewsletter, practitioner forms, membership and shopping continue through their existing providers, preserving live submissions and account access.
AccessibilityKeyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive image alternatives, reduced motion and properly labeled calendars support broader access.

Provider-managed functions

Bookings, purchases, forms and member accounts remain with their established services. No changes were made in Squarespace, and no test bookings, payments or customer submissions were made.
