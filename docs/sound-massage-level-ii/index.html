Sound Massage Level II

Sound Massage

Level II

What you'll need

  • We provide bowls and gongs for your practice sessions.

  • If you already have equipment, bring Heart, Univ., and Belly Bowl, plus #2 & #3 mallets. Fen gong 40-50cm. Zen bowl 9cm.

  • Notebook, optional (a manual is provided)

  • Wear comfortable clothes and bring socks, avoid jewelry

  • Lunch and snack, water for each day

5-10% discount on bowls purchased during seminar

Peter Hess® Sound Massage Level II

Join us for an exciting continuation of the Peter Hess® Sound Massage training module! In Level II, we will shift our focus from the physical body to the bio-energetic field, also known as the aura. This course will deepen your understanding and appreciation of this energy field, allowing you to expand your sensitivity to the effects of sound frequencies and vibrations. Throughout the training, you will also develop your mental focus and inner attitude, key components in the "power of consciousness" that underlies our method as Sound Massage practitioners. Don't miss out on this opportunity to refine your skills and take your practice to the next level!

Seminar is 2 full days. 

Participants receive attendance certificate. 15 CE’s available thru NCCAOM (National Certification Commission for Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine)

This seminar is a prerequisite for certification as a Peter Hess® Sound Massage Practitioner. Full program includes 4 levels of instruction, plus a supervision program.

Course Objective

The curriculum incorporates the use of therapy “grade” singing bowls and gongs where the student will learn how to work in the human energy field to clean, strengthen and harmonize the subtle body, strengthening health in the holistic sense —as without, so within and vice versa.

Upon completing this level, participants receive a certificate of completion.

  • Prerequisites: Level 1

  • Hours of Study: 16

  • Lecturer: Gina Armstrong

Course Description for Level II

  • The aura, the human energy field, and it’s significance in sound work.

  • Awareness and sensing of the energy field through our hands

  • Sensing of the energy field through the sound of singing bowls and gongs

  • Applications of the singing bowls and gongs in the aura, aura painting, and packing

  • Principles of grounding through sound

  • Cleansing, strengthening and harmonizing the aura with fen gongs

  • Practicing new elements with basic sound massage
