Level I

What you'll need

  • We provide bowls for your practice sessions

  • If you already have therapy bowls, bring Heart, Univ. and Belly Bowl with #2 and #3 mallets

  • Novelty/antique bowls are welcome for comparison only

  • Notebook, optional (a manual is provided)

  • Wear comfortable clothes and bring socks, avoid jewelry

  • Lunch and snack, water for each day

5-10% discount on bowls purchased during seminar

Peter Hess® Sound Massage Level I

Are you ready to tap into the transformative power of sound with Peter Hess® Sound Massage? Our introductory course is the perfect opportunity, open to anyone seeking new skills and powerful ways to promote wellness. Whether you're a seasoned wellness pro or simply a compassionate caregiver, this program offers a wealth of benefits for you and the people you care about. You'll leave the training feeling rejuvenated and empowered, ready to share your newfound knowledge with the world. And the best part? This course is just the beginning. With certification options and a range of advanced courses, you can take your skills to new heights and truly make a difference in the lives of others. Unlock the power of sound and join the revolution today!

Seminar is 2 full days. 

Participants receive attendance certificate. 15 CE’s available thru NCCAOM (National Certification Commission for Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine)

This seminar may be taken alone, and is a prerequisite for certification as a Peter Hess® Sound Massage Practitioner. Full program includes 4 levels of instruction, plus a supervision program. 

Course Objective

The goal of this training is to familiarize you, the participant, with the basic principles of Peter Hess® Sound Massage. Not only will you have the opportunity to experience the “Sound Massage” yourself, you will also receive your first instructions in sound massage applications and, as a result, you will be able to perform the “Sound Massage” for private/personal use.

  • Prerequisites: None

  • Hours of Study: 16

  • Lecturer: Gina Armstrong

Course Description for Level 1

  • History of “Sound Massage”

  • Sound massage theory

  • Demonstration of the basic sound massage

  • Experience the sensation of sound in the body

  • Guided practice of the basic sound massage in pairs

  • Independent practice of the sound massage in pairs

  • Group discussions

  • Information about various types of singing bowls and mallets

  • Application of the “Sound Massage” — possibilities, safety and limitations.
