PHI Registration

Application for Peter Hess® Seminars

Thank you for joining us – it’s our pleasure and honor to facilitate your learning in Peter Hess® sound methods.

Planning Your Visit:

Tip: For best results, clear your agenda for seminar days and plan to relax during break times.

Unless noted, participants arrange their own lodging, meals, and travel. Whenever we offer such services in connection with a specific seminar it will be under separate agreement.

Accept at your own risk any offers to share rooms, food, carpool rides, etc.

Check with us before purchasing flights or making other non-refundable arrangements

Expectations

  • Participants gain certificates by engaging in all lessons and attaining minimum proficiency.

  • In the rare event a certificate is withheld, facilitator outlines a plan of support for participant to obtain certificate (repeat seminars, shadowing, practice hours, practical tests, etc.)

  • Participants are expected to assess in advance their ability to stand for 1 hour; lift, balance, and bend/reach with weight; lie prone and supine for 30 min each; receive sound-massage on the body; follow verbal, visual, and written cues; and follow structured lessons within a group.

  • Participants agree to discuss with medical professionals any physical or psychiatric conditions or concerns which may represent difficulty or risk to themselves or others or limit ability to participate fully in the seminar(s).

  • Participants agree to disclose to facilitator right away if health and/or ability concerns exist.

  • Facilitator will suggest accommodations that preserve seminar integrity. If accommodation agreement is not reached (assuming timely notice) a refund or credit may be issued.

  • Participants take full responsibility for their own decisions and actions, and agree to resolve any arising difficulties clearly and immediately with the facilitator.

  • Participants agree not to practice other procedures (bodywork, reiki, counseling, alt. sound methods, etc) during the seminar unless directed by or expressly permitted by the facilitator.

  • The Peter Hess method is supportive and non-medical (though it may be applied by medical professionals). We do not diagnose, prevent, treat, or cure disease (no claim of “healing”).

Cancelation and Refund Policies:

Some planning expenses are unrecoverable, and cancellation refunds are limited accordingly:

  • Phase 1: up to 30 days prior to start of seminar – full refund less $500 admin fee (no fee if we cancel).

  • Phase 2: after deadline,– Seminar Credit less $500 admin fee (no fee if we cancel).

ADDITIONAL TERMS

  • Cancellation must be made in writing (get confirmation). Allow 14 days for any refund due.

  • Participant may be ejected without refund for violation of terms, inappropriate or disruptive behavior, or if further involvement represents a risk to self/others/venue/equipment.

  • If facilitator cancels late and no substitute instructor is available, any part of your deposit not recoverable for refund will be returned in the form of a voucher good for future seminars.

  • Peripheral costs are not refundable under this agreement (e.g. flights, meals, lodging, other).

  • For cancellation circumstances not listed here, accounts are handled case-by-case.

  • We prefer to address important issues in advance, so feel free to inquire if anything seems unclear. If something comes up later, tell us right away so we can find a solution together
