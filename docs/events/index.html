Seasonal Reset - December

Friday, January 12th 6-8pm

Relax and reset yourself by combining the amazing qualities of restorative yoga, self-myofascial release, mindfulness, and essential oils into one super healing and stress-relieving practice.  ALL LEVELS WELCOME

Join veteran yoga teacher, personal trainer, and corrective exercise specialist Rachael Erin Haas for a seasonal immersion to reset and restore the body and mind while connecting to healing spirit and community. Combining the therapeutic qualities of restorative yoga, self-myofascial release and mindfulness meditations with essential oils, learn ways to bring yourself back into balance releasing stress and prepare for the coming season. 

Self-myofascial release techniques using balls and rollers will be taught in coordination with yoga postures and stretches to amplify their effectiveness, reduce pain and discomfort, and increase flexibility and strength. Corresponding mindfulness meditations with an essential oil diffusing bracelet will follow to connect the mind and body. 

$48 includes handouts, foam roller, and essential oil diffusing bracelet, $5 off coupon for additional purchases

Yoga Nidra Sound Journey

  • Tuesday, April 25, 2023
  • 6:30 PM 8:00 PM
  • Soundspace (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

Yoga Nidra is the practice and art of Yogic Sleep. It is a profound but simple guided meditation accessible to anyone. The practice systematically guides the body, mind and breath into a complete state ofrelaxation. Quiet the mind and cultivate inner serenity as you rest in a supported savasana. Nancy’s voice will lead you through a guided relaxation where all the senses are drawn to gentle, conscious awareness,invitingthe body to heal, creativity to flourish,and heart to expand. While in this safe, sacred space Jeremy and Nancy will create a soundscape of handpan, didgeridoo, crystal singing bowls, multiple gongs, chimes and more. Each experience is created and offered with love and intention for healing and relaxation. Receive deep rest while being immersed in the ethereal vibrations of a Yoga Nidra Sound Journey.

All are welcome, no prior experience with yoga or meditation necessary.
