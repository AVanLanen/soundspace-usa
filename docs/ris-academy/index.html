From the warm drones of the Shruti Box and Harmonium to the rich resonance of the Monochord and the ethereal tones of the Handpan, this class will guide you in using these instruments for meditation, relaxation, and healing.

  • Gain practical experience in combining these instruments into a cohesive sound healing session.

  • Develop the confidence to incorporate these instruments into both personal meditation practices and group sound healing sessions.

By the end of this class, you will have foundational skills in playing each instrument and a deeper understanding of how they can enrich your sound healing toolkit.
