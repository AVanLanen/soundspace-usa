Upcoming Continuing Ed Credits for Massage Therapist Training

SoundBody Method™ for Massage Therapists

We’re excited to announce that the SoundBody Method™: Working with Singing Bowls on the Body! This 24-hour, in-depth continuing education course is designed for Licensed Massage Therapists who are ready to integrate therapeutic sound and vibration into their bodywork practice.

Students will learn to apply a structured, hands-on protocol using authentic Peter Hess® Singing Bowls, handcrafted in Germany specifically for therapeutic use. The training focuses on the intentional application of Singing Bowls—each selected for their unique frequency profile and vibrational impact on the body.

You’ll explore how sound supports nervous system regulation, fascial release, energetic balance, and deep relaxation—all within the ethical and professional scope of massage therapy. This course combines sound-based theory, guided meditations, and practical hands-on training in a heart-centered learning environment.

This course is NCBTMB-approved for 24 CE hours.
Space will be limited for this immersive training experience.

SoundBody Method™ – A Gentle Introduction to Vibrational Touch

The SoundBody Method™ beginner-friendly training in therapeutic sound and vibrational touch. Whether you’re completely new to offering one-on-one sessions or simply curious about how to support others through presence and sound, this immersive experience was designed with you in mind.

Over the course of 24 hours, you’ll learn how to confidently offer a structured protocol using the Peter Hess® Therapeutic Bowl Pro Set, carefully applied to the body to support relaxation, nervous system regulation, and energetic balance. You’ll be guided step-by-step in how to safely and respectfully work with sound through direct, body-centered application.

Each day blends meditative practice, hands-on learning, and thoughtful integration—no previous experience with massage, energy work, or sound healing is required. This training is rooted in presence, simplicity, and the belief that healing begins with listening.

Whether you feel called to hold space, deepen your connection to sound, or gently begin working one-on-one, the SoundBody Method™ offers a grounded, heart-led entry point into vibrational practice.
